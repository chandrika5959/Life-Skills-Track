# Learning Principles and Techniques

## Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a method of learning whereby you take a difficult concept and explain it in simple terms so that anyone can understand it.

## Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

**Active Mode:** that's when we're fully engaged in a task and trying to solve a problem. Writing Java code is a good example of this. So is preparing for an interview.

**Diffuse mode:** that's when we're relaxed and our minds are free to wander. Example: listening to music or going for a walk after you've been studying.

## Q4: According to the video, what are the steps to take when you're approaching a new topic?

1. Firstly, pick the topic you want to learn.
2. Set a clear objective as to what you're trying to achieve.
3. Break the subject down into small, manageable sections.
4. Gather the basic learning materials you'll need.
5. Start practising rather than just watching or reading.
6. Keep at it and improve by learning from your mistakes.
