
# Listening and Active Communication

## 1. Active Listening

### Question 1: What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

1. Pay attention to the person who is speaking.
2. Look at the person while they are talking.
3. Do not interrupt them in the middle of a conversation.
4. Listen carefully and try to understand what they are saying.
5. Ask questions if you don't understand anything.
6. Give a proper response to show that you are listening.

## 2. Reflective Listening

### Question 2: According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

Reflective listening means listening carefully and repeating what we understood in our own words.

The main points are:

1. Listen carefully without interrupting the other person.
2. Try to understand what they are saying.
3. Pay attention to their feelings and emotions.
4. Repeat the main points in your own words.
5. Ask questions if something is not clear.

In a company, we can use reflective listening during meetings. We can write down important points and share them with the team. This helps everyone understand the work clearly and avoid mistakes.

## 3. Reflection

### Question 3: What are the obstacles in your listening process?

Some problems I face while listening are:

1. Sometimes I get distracted easily.
2. I may start thinking about my reply before the other person finishes speaking.
3. Sometimes I don't understand a few English words.
4. When someone speaks very fast, I miss some points.
5. Sometimes I feel nervous while talking to new people.

### Question 4: What can you do to improve your listening?

1. I will listen carefully when someone is speaking.
2. I will avoid using my phone during conversations.
3. I will watch English videos and listen to podcasts.
4. I will try to learn new English words every day.
5. I will allow others to finish speaking before I respond.
6. I will ask questions whenever I have doubts.
7. I will practice listening during group discussions.

## 4. Types of Communication

### Question 5: When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?

Sometimes I use passive communication when I talk to new people or seniors. I feel shy or nervous to share my opinions. Even when I don't agree with something, I sometimes stay quiet because I don't want to hurt anyone or start an argument.

### Question 6: When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

Sometimes when I get angry or frustrated, I speak loudly without thinking about my words. This usually happens when things don't go as I expected. Later, I feel that I should have handled the situation calmly.

### Question 7: When do you switch into Passive Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

Sometimes when I feel bad about something, I don't tell the person directly. Instead, I stay silent, avoid talking, or make sarcastic comments. I know this is not a good way to solve problems. It is better to explain my feelings clearly.

### Question 8: How can you make your communication assertive?

I can improve my communication by following these steps:

1. Speak clearly and confidently.
2. Share my thoughts without feeling afraid.
3. Respect other people's opinions.
4. Say no politely when I cannot do something.
5. Stay calm when I am angry or upset.
6. Listen to others without interrupting them.
7. Use polite words while talking.
8. Explain my feelings instead of keeping them inside.
9. Think before speaking.
10. Practice speaking confidently with different people.

## Conclusion

I learned that good communication is not only about speaking. Listening and understanding others are also important. I will try to listen carefully, speak confidently, and respect everyone's opinions. I believe these small changes will help me improve my communication skills in daily life and at work.
